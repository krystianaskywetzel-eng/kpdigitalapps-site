How it works

Three steps, every week.

  1. i.

    Snap the list

    Take a photo of this week's sight word list from your child's teacher.

  2. ii.

    Swipe the cards

    Practice together with simple flash cards, one word at a time.

  3. iii.

    See what's mastered

    Know which words have truly stuck and which need another round.
