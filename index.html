About me

What I do

Three ways I help.

For families App building I design and build simple apps that do one job well. The first is Sight Word Buddy, which turns any child's sight word list into flash cards they can practice anywhere. More are on the way. See Sight Word Buddy
For small businesses AI automations I set up AI assistants that handle the busywork. For a local concrete company, that means bid files organized automatically and takeoffs and proposals drafted for the owner to review and send. I'm always learning what's new and setting up agents and workflows that make everyday work easier.
For communities & neighbors Websites & tech help I manage the website and tech for a conservation fund and serve as technology chair for a school PTO. I'm also the one people call when a parent or grandparent gets a new phone or computer, and I'm happy to help with setup and the basics.
