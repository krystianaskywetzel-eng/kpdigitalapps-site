About

Hi, I'm Krystiana.

I'm a mom, a wife, and a user first and foremost.

I didn't set out to work in tech. I ended up here by accident, and then I fell in love with this world. Most of what I know about software I learned from the other side of the screen: clicking through it, finding where it gets in the way, and wishing it just worked.

Family comes first. My son wrestles, and there's nothing I love more than watching him compete. My daughter plays volleyball, and I get to coach her team. My husband always has a new business venture going, and I'm right there with him, whether that means setting up the tech or talking through the next big idea.

When we get a break, we travel and we fish. Give us somewhere new and a little water, and we're happy.

I also volunteer as my school PTO's technology chair, so between the gym, the mat and the school, I spend a lot of time around busy families and the tools they juggle.

When something doesn't work the way I want it to, I start thinking about how I could build it better.

KP Digital is where I do that. I build small, simple tools for the everyday jobs that eat up busy people's time, and I'm always working on the next one.
